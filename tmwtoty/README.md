### How to Run

#### Naughty / Nice list

Players that are on the naughty or nice list are marked with the corresponding marker tokens. 
At the start of each new night, update the naughty list: Any player that has the "Night 1" marker gets a "Night 2" marker, any
player that has a "Night 2" marker and is in the good team is shrouded and dies. 
(This is subsequent to change and testing. If you would like to rule the naughty / nice list at the end of each night instead, allowing players on their
third night to use their ability one last time or still be saved, "Night 3" markers are provided as well.)


In order to access the markers on Pocket Grimoire, please add the corresponding fabled characters to your game.

#### Yuletide Zealot

A "gift" can be almost anything, but ideally, it should not just be straight-up information.
In any case, the player should have to do something or figure out the information on their own
based on what you said.

The gift does not need to indicate to the player whether they are on the nice, naughty or neither list.

Take for example the gift: "Today, the first time the Demon votes, it will not be counted."


If they are on the nice list, this should work as described.


If they are on the naughty list, you could instead, for example, pick a red herring to not count instead.


If they are drunk or poisoned, it should not work at all, meaning all votes should be counted as usual.


However, keep in mind that if the player is not on the nice list, the gift does not have to be bad for them, it just "might" be.
You can also use a bad gift to indicate to the player that they are on the naughty list, or give some information along with a drawback
if the good team is currently losing.


In general, the gift should only be solely helpful if the player is on the nice list. Otherwise, it could either be helpful with some drawback, misguiding or actively bad for the player, rather than their entire team.

#### Elf

The player that is chosen by the Elf is marked with the "Is An Elf" reminder token. **Their alignment does not change**. The drawback is
that they loose their ability gain the Elf ability instead, meaning that if the chosen player is now voted out, another player looses
their ability.

The evil team could also be hit with an Elf overriding a Minion ability, only the Demon does not loose their ability when chosen as an Elf. They are still shown that they were chosen by the previous Elf.

#### Saint Nicholas

When not counting a vote, it should not immediately be announced as to not indicate who is affected by the ability.
Instead, you should count the votes as you normally would, and then, when announcing the result, say:
"[Player] got X votes, Y of which were not counted, meaning that..."

This makes it easier for the evil team to tamper with votes and especially force ties on a slim majority. Additionally, it
might force the good team to get larger majorities or use ghost votes prematurely.

#### Rudolph

During setup, when Rudolph is in game, immediately mark all players with the "Naughty List - Night 1" marker. Proceed with updating the markers at the start of each night subsequent night as per usual.
